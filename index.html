What is this study?

This study isn't testing a new treatment, but rather focusing on understanding your experience. Researchers are working to ensure the best tools are available to accurately measure how having an acoustic neuroma���a non-cancerous inner ear nerve tumor���affects your daily life and comfort.

Who can participate?

You might be eligible if you have been diagnosed with an acoustic neuroma that is currently being watched with scans or has already been surgically removed. Importantly, you must be a native Italian speaker and cannot have certain other conditions, like a genetic disorder causing multiple tumors or prior head/neck radiation.

What will happen?

If you join, you will be asked to complete questionnaires (surveys) about your quality of life and how satisfied you feel with the decisions made about your health. This is an observational study, meaning it only involves answering questions, not receiving new medical procedures or drugs.

Where and when?

This research is currently looking for participants in Bologna, Italy, and is actively recruiting new people to join now.

Key Points

1This study is observational, focusing only on understanding your quality of life through surveys.
2The goal is to validate surveys in Italian so future patients can better track their well-being.
3You must be a native Italian speaker to participate.
